I · Opening

VIVARA began with a question about memory, the kind the earth keeps for us. Pressed into stone over centuries, then carried down from the mountains as resin.

From that question came an evening. At KRONOS in Japan, twenty-one guests gathered for the first enactment of VIVARA, a nine-course passage through the five elements rendered in food, sound, scent, light, and stillness.

Ancestral Futurism is the philosophy beneath it. Old intelligence carried into new ritual, new design, new ways of being in a body. The evening was built to be practiced, and at its center sat a single drop, dark as the mountains it came from, holding the quiet authority of things that have waited a long time to be remembered.

II · The Enactment

The First
Enactment

KRONOS, a dining room in Japan, was given over to darkness for one night. Black sand, obsidian surfaces, candlelight, and a single luminous sigil at the heart of the room.

Twenty-one guests were seated as participants in a ceremony shaped like a meal. Sound, scent, and light shifted with each threshold, so that the room itself seemed to breathe along the arc of the evening. The intention was quiet: to let people feel the philosophy in the body before they were ever asked to name it.

III · Living Alchemy

Living Alchemy

Shilajit is a remembrance. It forms slowly in the high rock of the Himalaya, a mineral record of plant life pressed and held across time, then drawn out as resin. To take it is to rejoin a long lineage of people who reached for the same dark material and trusted what it carried.

VIVARA holds this at the center of a daily ritual. Mineral intelligence on one side, human attention on the other, meeting for a moment each day. Ancestral Futurism names the bridge: ancient knowledge in future form.

IV · The Passage

Five Elements,
Nine Courses

The meal was written as a sequence of thresholds. Each element asked something different of the guest, and each course answered in food, vessel, and light.

ACT I地Earth

The evening began low and grounded. Roots and mineral salt, the cold scent of stone. Food that asked the guest to arrive fully in the body before anything else was offered.

ACT II水Water

Then came flow. A dark broth poured at the table, sea and flower suspended together, the surface moving in slow rings beneath candlelight.

ACT IV風Wind

The table lifted into air. Thin slices arranged like script, herbs that moved with the breath of the room, flavors almost weightless.

ACT V空Æther

At the edge of the void, sweetness and resin. A final plate marked with a sigil drawn in dark syrup, the circle of the evening quietly closing.

For Immediate Release
JAPAN

VIVARA debuts Element 003 with “Ancestral Futurism,” a nine-course ritual dinner in Japan

VIVARA, a premium shilajit ritual brand, held the first public enactment of its philosophy at KRONOS in Japan. Titled Ancestral Futurism, the evening gathered twenty-one guests for a nine-course journey through the five elements and marked the debut of Element 003, the brand’s shilajit Drop.

For one night, the dining room was given over to darkness, black sand, candlelight, and a single luminous sigil. The menu carried the arc of the evening, from grounding root and mineral through broth, ember, and air, to the Drop itself, offered into warmth and received in silence.

VIVARA positions shilajit as mineral memory and ancestral restoration rather than a supplement, a daily practice repeated quietly over time. Ancestral Futurism, the philosophy beneath the brand, describes ancient intelligence carried into modern ritual, design, and embodied culture.

The ceremony was realized in Japan with culinary artist Chuck Achieke and the KRONOS team, original music by Elijah Badua, a central light installation by XVISION, and cinematography led by Adrien Oniega. It was presented with support from Reality Center.
